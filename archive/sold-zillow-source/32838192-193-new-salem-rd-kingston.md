## What's special

Semi-Attached waterfront cottage. This a rare opportunity to own an affordable waterfront property just a five minute drive from the Rondout historic district. The waterfront location of this house is further enhanced by the established perennial gardens and expansive western views across the Rondout Creek. The gardens of this home and those of the neighbor are seamlessly integrated providing privacy and ambiance. Launch kayaks, enjoy morning coffee and sunsets, watch the movement of migratory birds and the changing seasons all from this waterfront oasis.

The cozy interior of the home receives both southern and western light with water views to the back of the house. A remote controlled gas fireplace in the living room further enhances the welcoming vibe of this historic Rondout Creek cottage. Enclosed porch, low taxes, 5 year old furnace, 8 year old roof, off street parking for two cars.
