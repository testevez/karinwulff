## What's special

Yes, THAT house is finally available. After nearly 60 years in the same family, this executive ranch on the view side of Kingston's Dietz Court is ready for its next chapter. Featuring one-level living with an unspoiled Mid-Century vibe, the house enjoys one of the most spectacular year-round views in all of the Hudson Valley, with a panoramic, 180-degree sight line of everything from the Hudson River south to the Rondout Creek, the picturesque old Wurts Street Bridge and the church steeples of the hot Rondout neighborhood, and then east all the way to the Berkshires. The .89-acre parcel extends all the way down to McEntee Street, allowing you to trim all trees as needed or open the view to even more visual drama.
