Built in 2016, this 4-bedroom mountain home sits on 2.8 acres at the top of
a quiet private road, with the kind of unobstructed western view that's
specific to this stretch of the Swannanoa Valley. The wraparound deck is the
heart of the property — covered on the rear, open on the sun side — and the
great-room glass extends the view inside on cold days.

Black Mountain proper is five minutes down the mountain: the Saturday morning
breakfast spots, the Lookout Brewing Co., the post office, the small but
genuinely nice independent bookstore. The Mountains-to-Sea Trail crosses the
ridge a quarter mile from the property line.
